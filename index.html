Welcome to Alder & Ember. We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise and keep the good table by the window free for whoever needs it.

A small room with a long memory.

Students come for the long tables and the outlets. Nearby workers come for a flat white at 8:15. Families take the corner booth on Saturdays, and visitors find us by the smell of the oven.

Everything here is made to be shared: a pastry split in two, a pot of filter coffee for the table, a conversation that runs over.
