Our story

Started with one machine and a borrowed oven.

Mara and Joel opened Alder & Ember in 2016 after years of working in other people’s kitchens.

They wanted a place where the coffee was taken seriously and the welcome was not. The first menu fit on one page. It still does.

Today a team of nine bakes, brews and washes up together, and most of the regulars know everyone by name.

What we care about

Coffee, roasted for taste

We buy from three farms we can name, roast in small batches, and taste every lot before it reaches the counter.

Ingredients with a face

Eggs, flour and dairy come from regional producers. Fruit follows the season, so the menu shifts with it.

Room to linger

No rush, no minimum spend, and a card by the till for anyone who wants to pay for a stranger’s coffee.

The experience

Walk in, and you’ll hear the grinder before you see the counter. Order at the bar, take the table that suits you, and we’ll bring the food over.

Children get a small cup of warm milk and a biscuit. Dogs get water. Laptops are welcome until the lunch rush.
